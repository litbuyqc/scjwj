Oopbuy shopping guide

A shortlist earns its place.

Use an Oopbuy product spreadsheet to keep evidence and assumptions in separate fields. An unanswered question should remain visible.

01 /

Define the item before searching

Write down the category, intended fit, preferred material, color, and budget. “Black relaxed cotton T-shirt” is a more useful starting point than an open-ended brand search.

02 /

Save the exact product and variant

Record the source link, selected color, selected size, and date checked. A category page or a screenshot alone may not identify the same variant later.

03 /

Mark missing information

Use “unknown” for an absent measurement, material composition, or return condition. Request clarification rather than filling the gap from a similar-looking item.

04 /

Compare the whole purchase

Weigh fit confidence, listing detail, applicable return terms, and estimated delivered cost together. The lowest displayed item price does not settle the decision.

Practical answers to common questions about spreadsheet research, sizing, branded references, and cost estimates.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Useful fields include category, source URL, measurements, material, listed price, and questions to resolve before ordering.

Does this page provide an official Oopbuy spreadsheet?

No. This page is an independent research guide and category directory. It does not provide an official Oopbuy spreadsheet, live inventory, or an ordering service. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS.

Can a spreadsheet confirm a branded product is authentic?

No. A spreadsheet entry, logo, product photograph, or quality-control image does not establish authenticity. Verify provenance and seller documentation separately.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

For clothing, compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item that fits you. For shoes, use the exact model’s size chart and check the measurement method. Do not assume that the same size label fits consistently across listings.

Does the budget estimator include all final charges?

The estimator adds only the values you enter. It does not retrieve live shipping quotes, exchange rates, taxes, duties, or platform fees. Confirm applicable charges separately before paying.
